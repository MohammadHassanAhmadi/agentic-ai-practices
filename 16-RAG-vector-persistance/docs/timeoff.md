# Time Off Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive twenty-five days of paid annual leave per year, in
addition to public holidays. Leave accrues monthly but may be taken in advance
within the same calendar year.

Up to five unused days carry over into the next year and must be used before the
end of March. Days beyond that are lost, so plan long holidays early rather than
stacking everything into December.

Requests are approved by your direct manager. A request longer than ten
consecutive working days should be submitted at least one month in advance, so
the team can plan cover.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and is not capped. Notify your manager
on the first day you are unwell. A medical certificate is required from the
fourth consecutive day onwards.

You are not expected to answer messages while on sick leave, and you should not
be assigned to on-call rotation during that period.

## On-call compensation

Engineers on the on-call rotation receive compensation for each week of duty, and
additional time off in lieu if they are paged outside working hours. Time in lieu
must be taken within the following month. Being on call does not change your
normal working hours; it is expected that you rest after a night-time page rather
than working a full day on top of it.

## Parental and unpaid leave

Parental leave follows the statutory minimum of the country of employment, with
the company topping up the salary to one hundred percent for the first sixteen
weeks. Unpaid leave of up to three months can be requested once every two years
and needs approval from both your manager and the people team.
